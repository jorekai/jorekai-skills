# Outreach templates

Plain text, under 120 words, one ask, the exact URL and sentence named. Subject lines are literal, not clever. Send from a personal address, follow up once after 7 days, then stop.

## Unlinked mention

Subject: `[Brand] on your [page topic] page`

> Hi [Name], thanks for mentioning [Brand] in "[page title]" ([URL]). One small thing: the mention has no link, so readers cannot get to it. Would you add one to [target URL] on the phrase "[exact phrase]"? Happy to return the favour on [your relevant page] if useful.
>
> [Name]

## Add to a roundup / listicle

Subject: `Missing entry for your "[list title]"`

> Hi [Name], your "[list title]" ([URL]) is the page I send people to for [topic]. One option that fits and is missing: [Brand], which [one-line difference, concrete]. [OWN NUMBER or proof]. If it belongs, here is a short blurb and a screenshot you can drop in: [blurb, 2 sentences].
>
> [Name]

## Resource suggestion (page cited by AI assistants or ranking for the query)

Subject: `Addition for "[page title]"`

> Hi [Name], "[page title]" ([URL]) shows up whenever I ask about [topic]. In the section on [section], readers currently get [gap]. [Target URL] covers exactly that with [evidence: numbers, screenshots, tested steps]. Would a link there make sense? I can also add [your page] as a reference to [their topic] from [your relevant URL].
>
> [Name]

## Guest post pitch

Subject: `Draft for [site]: [working title]`

> Hi [Name], [site] ranks for [query], and readers arriving there are asking [question you saw in comments / PAA]. I have a draft that answers it from experience: [working title], [word count], with [OWN NUMBER] and screenshots. One link to [target URL] where it is relevant, marked however you prefer. Want the draft?
>
> [Name]

## Paid placement (when value and exchange were declined)

Subject: `Sponsored link on "[page title]"`

> Hi [Name], understood. Would a sponsored placement work instead: one contextual link to [target URL] in [section], marked `rel="sponsored"`, for [amount]? Invoice from [company].
>
> [Name]
